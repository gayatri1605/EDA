📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes customer financial data to understand the factors that influence credit scores. Using Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), the goal is to uncover patterns in customer behavior, identify risk segments, and provide actionable insights for Paisabazaar to improve lending decisions and reduce default risk.

📝 Problem Statement
Paisabazaar faces challenges in accurately evaluating customer creditworthiness. Customers show varied financial behaviors — some maintain good discipline, while others struggle with high debt, delayed payments, or poor credit utilization. Without a strong data-driven approach, it is difficult to:

Identify high-risk customers early

Offer the right financial products to the right segments

Balance business growth with risk management

🎯 Business Objective
The objective is to use customer financial data to:

Identify key factors influencing credit scores (payment behavior, debt-to-income ratio, EMI-to-salary ratio, credit utilization).

Segment customers into risk categories (good, average, poor).

Reduce loan default risk by recognizing high-risk customers early.

Reward disciplined customers with premium offers and fair lending practices.

Support sustainable growth through data-driven decision-making.

🔍 Methodology
Data Cleaning & Preparation – Handling missing values, formatting variables, and preparing datasets.

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) – Using visualizations (bar charts, boxplots, scatter plots, pair plots) to uncover relationships between financial metrics and credit scores.

Insights Extraction – Identifying patterns such as:

Good payment behavior → higher credit scores

High debt-to-income ratio → lower credit scores

Balanced EMI-to-salary ratio → financial stability

Business Recommendations – Suggesting customer segmentation, risk management strategies, and product optimization.

📊 Key Findings
Payment discipline is the strongest driver of good credit scores.

High debt ratios and EMI burdens correlate with poor scores.

Moderate number of bank accounts and steady monthly balances indicate financial stability.

Frequent credit inquiries slightly lower scores, signaling risk-seeking behavior.

✅ Conclusion
EDA shows that disciplined payment habits, balanced debt ratios, and controlled credit utilization lead to good credit scores. Paisabazaar can use these insights to strengthen risk management, design better products, and support customers in improving their financial health.

🚀 Business Solution
Build predictive credit scoring models using behavioral and financial factors.

Segment customers into low, medium, and high-risk groups.

Offer premium products to good-score customers and counseling or restructuring to poor-score customers.

Continuously monitor and update models with new data for accuracy.
